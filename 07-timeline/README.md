# Learning Timeline

> Map section: [RL_LEARNING_MAP.md - Section 8](../RL_LEARNING_MAP.md#8-personalized-learning-timeline)

## What This Module Covers

Two structured plans: a 4-week crash course and a 12-week deep dive, each with weekly topics, resources, and hands-on tasks.

## 4-Week Crash Plan (20 hrs/week)

| Week | Focus | Hands-On |
|------|-------|----------|
| 1 | MDPs, Bellman, DP, MC, TD | Q-Learning on FrozenLake |
| 2 | DQN, Replay, Target Networks | DQN on CartPole |
| 3 | Policy Gradient, Actor-Critic, PPO | PPO on LunarLander |
| 4 | RLHF, DPO, GRPO | DPO fine-tuning with TRL |

## 12-Week Deep Plan (15-20 hrs/week)

| Week | Focus | Hands-On |
|------|-------|----------|
| 1-2 | Math foundations | NumPy exercises, gradient descent |
| 3-5 | Tabular RL (DP, MC, TD, Q-Learning) | Gridworld, Blackjack, FrozenLake |
| 6-7 | Deep RL (DQN + variants) | CartPole, Atari |
| 8-9 | Policy Gradient, PPO, Actor-Critic | LunarLander, MuJoCo |
| 10 | SAC, TD3, Continuous Control | HalfCheetah |
| 11-12 | RLHF, DPO, GRPO | LLM alignment project |

## Suggested Exercises

- Follow one plan consistently
- Track progress in a learning journal
- Adjust pace based on understanding, not calendar
